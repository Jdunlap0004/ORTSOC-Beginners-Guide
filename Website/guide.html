The Beginner's Guide to ORTSOC:

Here is our comprehensive guide for ORTSOC students. We organized all of guide information into a pdf for students to easily click through and use. The guide has a basic overview of how to succeed in ORTSOC and then it goes into smaller guides for each rotation and the tools that they use. For a few scenarios, we created demonstration videos that are included to the right side of this page or at the end of the Beginner's Guide to ORTSOC file.

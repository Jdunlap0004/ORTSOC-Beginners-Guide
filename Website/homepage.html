Welcome to The Beginner's Guide to ORTSOC!

The Beginner's Guide to ORTSOC is a resource for Cybersecurity students starting ORTSOC. As ORTSOC students ourselves, experiencing the program for the first time was overwhelming and we were unprepared. Our goal with this guide is to set incoming ORTSOC students up for success. Our guide includes the written guide itself, our documentation for the guide, and demonstration videos.

What is ORTSOC?

The Oregon Research & Teaching Security Operations Center is a three-term program for Oregon State University Cybersecurity students. ORTSOC is a chance for students to experience what it's like to be in a real-life SOC by attending shift 11 hours per week. The clients that students provide Cybersecurity services for are small public, not-for-profit, or educational organizations that can't afford their own Cybersecurity team or services.
